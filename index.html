Quick answers

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad (coming soon to the App Store) and Mac (available now, macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel).

Does it collect my typing?

No. Nothing you type is stored, logged or sent anywhere. The Mac keyboard never connects to the internet and the iPhone keyboard needs no Full Access.

How do I install the Mac version?

Download the disk image, open it, drag the app to Applications, press Install, then add the keyboard in System Settings. The guide has every step.
